How it works

No inventory. No upfront cost. No getting stiffed.

1

Post a "find"

Item, size, photo, your max price, and the finder's fee. Everything spelled out up front — no DMs, no guessing.

2

Finders browse open requests near them and claim what they can get. They front the cost and snap a photo.

3

Escrow releases on proof

The buyer's funds sit safe in escrow. On confirmed handoff, the Finder is paid back plus their fee.
