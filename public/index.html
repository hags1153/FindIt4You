How it works

No inventory. No upfront cost. No getting stiffed.

1

Buyers post a "find"

Item, size, a photo, your max price, and how much you'll pay a Finder to grab it. Everything is spelled out up front — no DMs, no guessing.

2

Finders claim & shop

Finders browse open requests near them and claim the ones they can find. They front the cost, snap a photo, and lock it in.

3

Escrow releases on proof

The buyer's payment sits safely in escrow. Once the item is confirmed and handed off, the Finder gets paid back plus their finder's fee.

Why it's safe (and why it wins vs. the Facebook groups)

🔒

Escrow by default

Money is held the second a find is claimed and only releases on proof of the item. Nobody sends cash into the void.

⭐

Ratings & verified Finders

Every Finder builds a reputation. Buyers see completed finds and reviews before they trust someone.

📸

Photo proof

Finders show the actual item and receipt before funds release. What you see is what you get.

⚖️

Clear structure

Price caps, deadlines, and finder's fees are agreed up front — so there's nothing to argue about later.
